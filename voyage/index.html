A basement shop that never stopped growing

The Dawn Treader has bought and sold books in Ann Arbor, and shipped them around the world, for more than forty-five years.

Bill Gillmore opened it in 1976 in a tiny basement. Today it’s a roomy storefront on East Liberty Street with more than 70,000 titles, priced from fifty cents to many thousands of dollars.

Since 2022 it has been owned by Africa Schaumann, its manager of four years before that. Her specialty is rare and antiquarian books, and she is trained in book conservation and preservation.

Asimov’s Guide to Shakespeare

Isaac Asimov

Better known for science fiction, Asimov wrote this companion for his own Signet Classic Shakespeare. It explains the history, legend and myth behind all 38 plays and both narrative poems, sorted by where each play is set rather than by comedy, history and tragedy.

Selling your books

Retiring, moving, or out of shelf space? We pay cash, check or store trade for books published between 1440 and today.

  1. Call ahead

    Ring 734-995-1008 and tell us roughly how many boxes you have. We’ll suggest a good time, usually the same day.

  2. Bring them in

    We have a handcart and usually someone to help unload. Monday to Thursday, 11 to 4, is easiest for parking; Friday and Saturday are our busiest days.

  3. Get an offer

    Often in about 15 minutes, and within a day for a big lot. Offers depend on condition, demand and what we already stock.

  4. Take cash, check or trade

    Clean copies without highlighting or notes are worth more. We can’t take books with mold, pests or heavy damage.

House visits

For roughly ten boxes or more, we come to you, usually within 50 miles of Ann Arbor. We pack and load the books ourselves and leave a check the same day.
