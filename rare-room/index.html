Ann Arbor’s best browse

For more than forty-five years the Dawn Treader has bought and sold books in Ann Arbor and around the world. It started in a tiny basement. Today it fills a roomy storefront on East Liberty Street with more than seventy thousand titles.

Africa Schaumann’s expertise is rare and antiquarian books, and she is trained in book conservation and preservation. So the shelves run from this week’s paperbacks to books that have survived five centuries, at prices from fifty cents to many thousands of dollars.

Book of the week

Chosen July 27, 2026

Asimov’s Guide to Shakespeare

Isaac Asimov

Better known for science fiction, Asimov wrote this as a companion to his own Signet Classic Shakespeare. It isn’t criticism. It fills in the history, legend and myth behind all 38 plays and both narrative poems, and it groups them by where they’re set, Greek, Roman, Italian or English, rather than by comedy, history and tragedy.

Shelves full? We pay cash or trade.

Retiring, moving or just out of room, bring your books to the shop Ann Arbor has trusted with them for over forty-five years. We buy single books, whole libraries and collections, in every subject, from early printing to this year’s titles.

We make offers in person, based on condition, demand and what’s already on our shelves. Clean copies without highlighting or notes are worth more. We can’t take books with mold, pest or heavy water damage.

Walk in

Come by during opening hours. An offer often takes about 15 minutes, and up to a day for a big lot. We have a handcart and usually someone to help unload.

Parking out front is easiest Monday to Thursday, 11 to 4. Friday and Saturday are our busiest days. Bringing more than five boxes? Call first.

House visits

For roughly ten boxes or more, we come to you. We usually travel within 50 miles of Ann Arbor, and have gone as far as the Upper Peninsula and Indiana for the right books.

We box and load everything ourselves and leave you a check the same day.

Appraisals

Book an appointment and the owner will appraise one to five rare books free of charge.

You get a written note on what each book is, what it’s worth and how to look after it if you keep it.
